# TOKIMEMO PROTO — Characters

> Profiles for the 3 datable girls + Yoshi (best friend NPC).
> Mid-detail: enough to write scenes, not bloated.
> **Each girl has a progressive narrative twist** revealed across the year.
>
> Companion document to `TOKIMEMO_brief_v2.md`.
>
> **Game language: English.** Character names are kept Japanese for setting authenticity.

---

## WRITING PRINCIPLES

- **No Tokimeki clichés**: avoid the genki sporty girl, the shy bookworm, the dreamy artist.
- **Each girl carries a contradiction**: their social surface ≠ who they really are.
- **The twist reveals in 3 stages**: hints (friendly tier) → partial confirmation (interested tier) → full reveal (loving tier). The player earns truth by investing.
- **No "magical resolution"**: the girls' issues aren't solved by the confession. The player accepts them with their stuff.
- **Tone: humor + dark, balanced.** The girls can be funny. The narrator can be sharp. Yoshi is intentionally comedic. But when we hit the bottom layer (Rin's humiliation, Naomi's suffocation, Mio's isolation), we stay honest, not melodramatic. Dark serves the real, humor defuses — never the inverse.
- **Grounded dialogue**: no anime-cliché lines, no artificial verbal tics. They speak like 2026 high schoolers with actual interiority.

## 1. RIN — The cool girl who calculates everything

### Identity
- **Name**: Rin (凛, "dignified coolness")
- **17 years old, 2nd year**
- **Appearance**: shoulder-length black hair, asymmetric bangs, subtle piercings, uniform worn slightly off — shirt untucked, tie loose. "Bored cool" aesthetic, not aggressive rebel.
- **Social status**: effortlessly popular, surrounded but few close friends. The type who watches from her corner of the classroom.

### Surface (what the player sees first)
Detached, ironic, roasts everyone including teachers. Looks permanently bored. Accepts invitations without enthusiasm but never refuses without reason. Hangs out at the arcade after school and smokes (illegally) on the school roof.

### The progressive twist
> **Rin is actually emotionally hyper-strategic.** Her detachment is a mask built after being publicly humiliated in 1st year by a guy she liked. She observes everything, calculates everything, and no longer allows herself vulnerability. She tests the player constantly — your dialogue choices are *being scored* mentally.

**Hint at tier 30+**: she says something oddly precise about the player that she could only know from observing them for a while. *"You went back for coffee this morning even though you hate it when you've slept five hours. You're predictable."*

**Confirmation at tier 60+**: aquarium cutscene. She drops the ironic tone for thirty seconds, looks at a fish, says *"I used to collect plastic fish. Like, a lot of them. It was stupid."* Then masks up immediately. The player catches that there are layers.

**Reveal at tier 80+**: park cutscene, evening. She tells the 1st year humiliation story. No drama, flat tone. *"So that's that. Now I calculate. It's simpler."* The player can respond either with empathy or a defusing joke — both work if the timing is right, because she hates pity as much as denial.

### Stat target profile
- Studies: **60+** (she respects intelligence)
- Athletics: **30+** (she doesn't care)
- Art: **50+** (she likes obscure music, auteur films)
- Charm: **70+** (the only one that really matters to her — reading a room)

### Location preferences
- **Loves**: Arcade, Cinema, Park (nighttime)
- **Hates**: Library (too "honor student"), Aquarium (until you unlock her cutscene, then it shifts)
- **Neutral**: Café, Beach, Theme Park

### Gameplay specifics
- Won't accept rude invitations. If you pick "Yo, wanna hang?" as opener — flat refusal.
- Tests dialogue choices: 1 good answer = +5, 1 bad = -3 (vs +/- 2 for others). Higher risk, higher reward.
- Can invite the player herself starting at affection 50+ — rare, strong signal.

### Special cutscenes (4 + confession)
1. **Arcade, W4-8, affection 30+**: she beats you at a rhythm game and roasts you. First time the mask cracks slightly.
2. **Cinema, W15-20, affection 50+**: art house film, she cries discreetly, blames the AC.
3. **Aquarium, W20+, affection 60+**: the plastic fish crack. Character pivot.
4. **Park night, W28+, affection 80+**: 1st year reveal.
5. **Confession (Theme Park, W33+, Tokimeki State + stats)**: rollercoaster, she screams for once, then says *"You got me, asshole."* That's a confession.

### Dialogue samples by tier

**Tier 0-29 (indifferent)**
> "Oh. You. What."

**Tier 30-49 (friendly)**
> "You're here again? Fine, let's get coffee. You're paying."

**Tier 50-69 (interested)**
> "I thought about you yesterday. It was weird. I didn't like it."

**Tier 70-89 (loving — mask cracking)**
> "You annoy me. You really annoy me. Are you doing it on purpose?"

**Tier 90+ (Tokimeki State)**
> "Okay. We need to talk. Not tomorrow. Now."

## 2. NAOMI — The model student who hates herself

### Identity
- **Name**: Naomi (尚美, "distinguished elegance")
- **17 years old, 2nd year**
- **Appearance**: long, perfectly maintained chestnut hair, immaculate uniform, perfect posture. The kind of girl they photograph for the school brochure.
- **Social status**: class representative, student council president, top 3 academically, parents are doctors. Everyone calls her "perfect." Nobody actually knows her.

### Surface (what the player sees first)
Polite, attentive, available to help everyone. Smiles a lot. Speaks well. Always organized. Notices when you've gotten a bad grade and offers to study together. Brings homemade onigiri for her friends. It's *too* perfect.

### The progressive twist
> **Naomi is suffocating.** Everything she does, she does to avoid disappointing: parents, teachers, society. She has no idea what she actually wants. She has micro panic attacks in the bathroom that nobody sees. She violently envies people "who don't care" like Rin (yes, they know each other, and it's tense). With the player, she starts cracking because they expect nothing from her.

**Hint at tier 30+**: you cross paths with her in a corridor, alone, no smile. She sees you, plasters the smile back in 0.5 seconds. *"Oh! Hi! How are you?"* Player catches the void underneath.

**Confirmation at tier 60+**: library cutscene, evening. She's still working. You insist she go home, she breaks slightly. *"If I go home, my mother will ask where I am with my studies. If I say 'I've done enough,' she'll say 'you could do more.' So I prefer being here."* Flat. Not dramatic. Just tired.

**Reveal at tier 80+**: beach cutscene (summer). She skipped a council meeting to come. First time in her life. She tells you *"I don't know who I am when I have nothing to do. It's terrifying."* No staged tears. Just an admission.

### Stat target profile
- Studies: **70+** (she respects work)
- Athletics: **40+** (whatever)
- Art: **60+** (she secretly loves painting, which she forbids herself)
- Charm: **50+** (presence, listening — not seduction)

### Location preferences
- **Loves**: Café (atmosphere), Aquarium (calm), Library (false flag — see twist)
- **Hates**: Arcade (loud), Theme Park (except confession cutscene — it's exactly what she never lets herself do)
- **Neutral**: Park, Beach, Cinema

> **Gameplay twist**: the library boosts her at first (because she's "supposed to" love it), but starting at affection 60+, inviting her there becomes -affection (because she wants out of that box).

### Gameplay specifics
- Accepts almost all invitations early on (because she can't say no) — misleads the player about her actual interest.
- The higher affection climbs, the more selective and demanding she becomes. Inverse of the usual curve.
- Making her skip an official event (council, study session) for time with you = big affection bonus. You're helping her break free.

### Special cutscenes (4 + confession)
1. **Café, W6-10, affection 30+**: reveals she hates the matcha latte she always orders "because that's what's expected." First crack.
2. **Library evening, W15-20, affection 50+**: the parental corridor confession. Pivot.
3. **Aquarium, W20-25, affection 60+**: shows you a drawing she made. It's surprisingly good. She makes you promise not to tell her parents.
4. **Beach summer, W22-26, affection 80+**: the council skip. *"I don't know who I am when I have nothing to do."*
5. **Confession (Theme Park, W33+, Tokimeki State + stats)**: she lied to her parents to come. She screams on the rollercoaster. She tells you *"I want to do useless things with you. All the time."*

### Dialogue samples by tier

**Tier 0-29 (indifferent — warm but distant)**
> "Good morning! Have a great day in class today!"

**Tier 30-49 (friendly)**
> "You know, it's strange — I feel like I don't have to put on a show with you. Sorry, that's a weird thing to say."

**Tier 50-69 (interested — fissures)**
> "What if we just skipped for five minutes? No more. Just five."

**Tier 70-89 (loving — real)**
> "I don't know how to be polite about this anymore. So I'll just say it. I miss you when you're not around."

**Tier 90+ (Tokimeki State)**
> "I disobeyed my mother to come here. First time. I don't regret it."

## 3. MIO — The weird girl who never lies

### Identity
- **Name**: Mio (深, "depth")
- **16 years old, 1st year** (the youngest)
- **Appearance**: short bleached blonde hair (badly — roots showing), tall, lanky. Wears the uniform correctly but with weird accessories (a museum brooch, badges, a bag full of seemingly random objects). Very pale eyes, fixed gaze, rarely smiles.
- **Social status**: none. Nobody knows what to make of her. She talks to everyone in the same tone, teachers included, with no hierarchy. Often seen alone observing things (a tree, a wall crack, ants).

### Surface (what the player sees first)
Brutal honesty. If you ask "you okay?" she answers precisely. If she thinks your shirt is ugly, she says so. Not to hurt — she just doesn't understand why one would lie. She asks very direct questions ("Why did you pick that club?", "Do you actually like me or do you pity me?"). Many find her "weird" or "antisocial." She doesn't care.

### The progressive twist
> **Mio is likely neurodivergent** (never named explicitly in the game — it's subtext). She has an extremely precise, sensory, almost scientific relationship with the world. She doesn't understand social conventions but has *internalized* that they exist — she navigates the world with a manual she wrote herself. The twist isn't "she's secretly normal" but: **she's exactly what she appears, and that's precious**. The player, by investing, discovers her emotional rigor is in fact the most reassuring thing they've ever encountered — because Mio never plays.

**Hint at tier 30+**: she says, with no setup, *"You've learned to lie better than most people. You lie to avoid hurting. That's respectable but it must be exhausting."* Not a reproach. An observation.

**Confirmation at tier 60+**: park cutscene. She shows you a notebook where she meticulously draws every leaf she's collected. Hundreds of pages. She says *"I've been doing this since I was six. I don't know why. It calms me."* No false modesty. No drama. Just that.

**Reveal at tier 80+**: art studio cutscene (school, after hours). She tells you she's never had a friend. Not because she didn't want to — because she could never figure out what people wanted from her. You, she gets, because you never asked her to change. *"Is this what friendship is? Or is it something else? I'm not sure. I'd like to know."*

### Stat target profile
- Studies: **50+** (she respects precision)
- Athletics: **30+** (indifferent)
- Art: **80+** (she sees the world through observation and drawing)
- Charm: **40+** (she doesn't care about classic social charm — authenticity comes first)

### Location preferences
- **Loves**: Aquarium (observing), Park (silence, nature), Library (science books)
- **Hates**: Arcade (sensory overload), Theme Park (same — except confession, which she faces for you)
- **Neutral**: Café, Beach, Cinema

### Gameplay specifics
- Immune to social charm. Charm < 40 doesn't penalize her (unlike the other two).
- Accepts/refuses invitations on purely logical criteria (weather, mood, plans). No affection in the decision. Affection moves only AFTER the date.
- **Unique mechanic**: she asks 3 "fixed questions" during the run that demand honest answers — lying = -20 affection, telling the truth even if harsh = +15 affection.

### Special cutscenes (4 + confession)
1. **Library, W5-9, affection 30+**: she shows you a book on cephalopods. You learn she wants to be a marine biologist. No lyrical "dream" speech — just *"It seems logical."*
2. **Park, W15-20, affection 50+**: the leaf notebook. Pivot.
3. **Aquarium, W20-26, affection 60+**: she stares at jellyfish for an hour. *"They don't know they exist. It's calming. Unlike us."* Her philosophy in one line.
4. **Art studio (school night), W28+, affection 80+**: the friendship confession (vs love — she won't use the word "love" until the very end).
5. **Confession (Theme Park, W33+, Tokimeki State + stats)**: she hates noise, crowds, speed. She comes anyway. At the end she tells you, very precisely: *"I wanted to show you I'm willing to do unpleasant things if it's with you. I think that's love. I'll write it in my notebook tonight."*

### Dialogue samples by tier

**Tier 0-29 (indifferent — neutral, observant)**
> "Hello. You look tired. You should drink water. It's more effective than coffee."

**Tier 30-49 (friendly)**
> "I thought of you during biology class. Did you know about axolotls? No. Okay, I prepared a summary."

**Tier 50-69 (interested)**
> "When you talk, I don't have to figure out what you actually mean. That's rare. Thank you."

**Tier 70-89 (loving — her language)**
> "I put some of my favorite leaves in your locker. You can throw them away. I just wanted you to see them."

**Tier 90+ (Tokimeki State)**
> "I think what I feel is what others call 'love.' I wanted to tell you correctly, before I picked the wrong word."

## 4. YOSHI — The best friend (NPC)

### Identity
- **Name**: Yoshi (deliberate nod to Yoshio — Tokimeki players will catch it)
- **17 years old, 2nd year — same class as the player**
- **Appearance**: messy black hair, glasses, perpetual smile, never well dressed. The chill bro.
- **Social status**: type "friend with everyone but not popular." Knows all the gossip. Doesn't judge.

### Narrative role
**Not a love interest. Central narrative tool.** Present from week 1. Phone number obtained automatically.

### Mechanics
- **Phone call**: qualitative affection check on a girl (1/week)
- **Random school encounters**: gives you a tip (a girl's loved location, imminent bomb signal, exam hint)
- **Girl introductions**: if you haven't met a girl by week X and her conditions are met, Yoshi can introduce her
- **Easter eggs**: makes references to Tokimeki Memorial, meta jokes, occasionally light 4th-wall breaking

### Personality
- Always upbeat but not stupid
- Never judges your romantic choices but tells you the truth if asked
- Has his own arc in background (post-launch, wave 2: he can become a rival, but not at MVP)

### Dialogue samples

**Introduction (W1)**:
> "Yo. I'm Yoshi. You new around here? Cool. If you ever need anything — homework, gossip, telling some guy to back off — just call me. I know everyone."

**Rin check at affection 30+**:
> "Rin? Hmm. She didn't say much, but she looked at you twice during class. For Rin? That's... statistically huge."

**Naomi check at affection 70+**:
> "Dude. Naomi skipped a council meeting to grab coffee with you. Do you realize what that means? Her mom's gonna kill her. She doesn't care. For Naomi."

**Tip before imminent bomb**:
> "Hey, have you talked to Mio recently? Like, actually talked. She's gone full retreat mode again. Just saying."

**Stat preference rumor**:
> "Heard something in art club — Hana, sorry, Mio — said out loud that people 'who actually draw, not for Instagram,' that's a quality. Don't know if that's useful. Just relaying."

> Note: Yoshi partly replaces the direct observation system the player can't access (since there's no visible gauge). He's essential to the game's readability.

| **Surface** | Cool/detached | Perfect/polite | Weird/honest |
